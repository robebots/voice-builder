## Every draft

1. Pick the register from the recipient and channel. If it's unclear, ask.
2. Write in that register's style below.
3. Run the unslop pass in `references/unslop.md`, except for the habits under "Keep anyway".
4. Read it once as {{First}}. Cut anything they wouldn't actually say.
5. Hand over the draft with no commentary. Drafting isn't sending: {{First}} sends.

Never invent facts, opinions, promises, or feelings. Keep {{First}}'s level of certainty and the actual ask. If something's missing, leave `[[check]]`.
